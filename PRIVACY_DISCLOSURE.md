# Commercial Privacy Disclosure Statement
**Last Updated: August 8, 2026**

BizStack Perks Hub ("we," "our," or "us") operates the automated corporate matching routing interfaces, data capture pipelines, and analytics dashboard tools ("Services"). This disclosure explicitly details how business financial profile parameters are evaluated, stored, and verified for commercial underwriting lines.

## 1. Information We Collect
To process commercial underwriting assessments and facilitate corporate card finding linkages, we collect the following business infrastructure data parameters directly from corporate officers:
* **Corporate Entity Parameters:** Legal Business Name, Trade Status, Operational Type.
* **Underwriting Performance Metrics:** Estimated Gross Annual Revenues, Core Processing Inquiries.
* **Risk Classifications:** Executive Credit Rating Tier Brackets.
* **Geographic Assets:** Verified Corporate Physical Street Addresses (e.g., 701 Dana Drive, Chesapeake, VA 23321).

## 2. Secure Processing and Tokenization
* **Payment Gateways:** All premium service fee checkouts ($49.00 USD membership transactions) are fully tokenized and transmitted directly to certified merchant network aggregators (Stripe) over secure transport channels. We do not ingest, log, or maintain raw consumer credit card numbers or private CVV data layers in our internal database files (`bizstack.db`).
* **Relational Storage Safety:** Corporate underwriting leads and tracking link logs are isolated locally on secured node layers.

## 3. Data Transfers and External Sharing
We transfer business profile metrics exclusively when explicitly requested by the corporate user during link interaction sequences. Data rows are shared with verified financial networks (such as American Express, Chase Business Networks, or Plaid data systems) solely to process pre-qualification algorithms. We do not sell or trade private business metadata to third-party consumer advertising aggregators.
